## Toronto City Hall weddings in 2027 at a glance

**What stops:** Ceremonies in the Wedding Chamber at 100 Queen Street West, from January 1, 2027. The operator's lease ends December 31, 2026.

**What does not change:** 2026 ceremonies at City Hall go ahead as scheduled. Old City Hall bookings are not affected. The marriage licence counter at City Hall is a separate City service, and the City still lists it.

**If you hold a booking:** Email the City at marriage@toronto.ca. Also call the operator, Ministerial Associates, at 416-363-0316, because it holds the deposits, not the City.

**Other options for 2027:**

- Old City Hall with a City officiant: $566.25 including HST.
- North York Civic Centre with a City officiant: $441.95 including HST.
- A civic centre room with your own officiant: $136.85 including HST per 30 minutes.
- Anywhere with permission, with your own officiant: no published officiant price.
- A nearby town hall, for example Oakville: $413 including HST.

**Date checked:** November 20, 2026.

## What is happening at Toronto City Hall?

The City calls it a pause. CP24 reported that ceremonies "are being paused starting on Jan. 1" as the City moves ahead with plans for major construction and workplace modernization.

The ceremonies were run by a private company, Ministerial Associates, which opened in 1994 and has married couples at City Hall for 32 years. Its lease ends December 31, 2026.

The two sides tell it differently. The City says it offered a short lease extension to March 31, 2027, and the company declined. The company says the City "did not wish" to renew, and that it could not accept a three month extension.

The City says it will keep exploring options for wedding ceremonies at City Hall, with no date given. As of November 20, 2026, no replacement has been announced.

When I checked on November 20, 2026, the operator's own website still showed no closure notice and still listed its prices. So do not book a 2027 date from a website. Confirm directly.

## If you already booked City Hall

**A 2026 date:** Nothing changes. No source names the final ceremony day, so I can only say ceremonies run through the end of 2026.

**A 2027 date:** Email marriage@toronto.ca. The City says it will work with affected couples to find another downtown location, which could include Old City Hall, St. Lawrence Market North and other City venues, where possible.

**Your deposit:** The City does not hold deposits. Ask Ministerial Associates. I found nothing published about refunds, so I do not know how that will be handled.

## Where can you get married in Toronto in 2027?

### Old City Hall, with a City officiant

![David and Alvina walk hand in hand across Nathan Phillips Square, the Old City Hall clock tower and downtown towers behind them, by AD Photography](../../assets/images/portfolio/david-alvina/14.jpg)

Old City Hall is the sandstone building with the clock tower at 60 Queen Street West. The City holds civil weddings in its former Council Chamber.

**Fee:** $566.25 including HST for a 30 minute civil wedding in 2027. That is a $136.85 deposit when you reserve, plus a $429.40 officiant fee at a virtual consultation one week before. Both include HST. You need your licence before that consultation.

**Officiant:** A City officiant only. Outside officiants are not permitted.

**People:** Up to 25.

**Days:** Select weekdays, 10 AM to 3:30 PM.

**Booking:** Online, first come, first served. Dates open weekly, six months ahead, and there is no waiting list. It is still a pilot program.

**Witnesses:** Bring two, or the City can provide one for $30.40 including HST per witness.

**Photos inside:** Up to 30 minutes after the ceremony, in designated areas. All camera gear must be hand held and battery operated.

I have not photographed a ceremony inside this chamber, so this is what the City publishes. Outside, I know it well: David and Alvina crossed the square with its clock tower behind them just after noon. My [Old City Hall wedding guide](/blog/old-city-hall-wedding-toronto/) covers the building and its history. Its fees are from the middle of 2026, so use the 2027 fee on this page.

### North York Civic Centre, with a City officiant

This is the only other place where the City supplies the officiant. It is at 5100 Yonge Street.

**Thursdays and Fridays:** 10 AM to 3 PM, $441.95 including HST for 30 minutes. Get your licence first, then book a free consultation.

**Select Saturdays:** 10 AM to 4:30 PM, $481.50 including HST, non refundable. Request a date at marriage@toronto.ca and allow up to 3 business days for a reply. The 2026 Saturdays are full.

The City shows both prices with no year next to them, so confirm the 2027 price before you pay.

### A civic centre room, with your own officiant

Three City chambers rent as a room only:

- North York Civic Centre, 5100 Yonge St: up to 40 people.
- Scarborough Civic Centre, 150 Borough Dr: up to 22 people.
- York Civic Centre, 2700 Eglinton Ave W: up to 25 people.

**Fee:** $136.85 including HST per 30 minutes, non refundable. Bring your own officiant.

**Hours:** Every day, including weekends and holidays. Book online or in person.

**Rules:** No confetti or rice, no open flame, no food or alcohol.

I have not photographed inside these rooms, so these are the City's published details.

### Anywhere you like, with your own registered officiant

Once you have a licence, you can marry anywhere, with permission from the owner or tenant. The City's examples include your home, a restaurant, a banquet hall, a place of worship and a municipal park.

Ontario's "Getting married" page on ontario.ca lists who can marry you. That means a registered officiant recognized by their religious body or by an Indigenous community or entity. For a civil ceremony, it can be a municipal clerk or their delegate, a judge, a justice of the peace, or a Member of Provincial Parliament who has given written notice to the Minister. To check an officiant is registered, use the ServiceOntario search linked from that page.

I found no reliable published price range for officiants in Toronto, so ask each one for a written quote.

Outside City Hall, this is the option I have photographed most, each with an independent officiant: Abdullah and Nadia at the [Toronto Music Garden](/portfolio/abdullah-nadia/), Nidhi and Derek at [Guild Park and Gardens in Scarborough](/portfolio/nidhi-derek/), and Harsh and Payal on the [Toronto Islands](/portfolio/harsh-payal/). ## Two reality checks

**St. Lawrence Market North is a hall, not a ceremony room.** It must be rented for the full day. The main floor is listed at $4,500, and the page does not say whether HST is added. It is not available on Saturdays because of the farmers' market. The City named it only as a possible spot for couples whose City Hall bookings are affected.

**A courthouse wedding is legal, but not bookable.** Judges and justices of the peace can marry people in Ontario. But I found no public booking service for courthouse weddings in Toronto, and Ontario's own advice is to contact a local city hall or town hall. Do not plan on walking into a courthouse.

## The marriage licence

**Cost:** $180 from the City of Toronto. HST does not apply.

**Valid:** Three months from the day it is issued, anywhere in Ontario.

**Where:** By appointment only, at Toronto City Hall, 100 Queen Street West, first floor, or the North York Civic Centre, 5100 Yonge Street. Walk in clients are not accepted.

**ID:** Two pieces of original, unexpired government ID for each of you, four in total. Health cards and SIN cards are not accepted. Only one of you has to attend.

**On the day:** Two witnesses, physically present at the ceremony.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Toronto City Hall closed for weddings in 2027?**

Ceremonies in the Wedding Chamber are paused from January 1, 2027. 2026 bookings go ahead.

**What is the closest option?**

Old City Hall: $566.25 including HST in 2027, City officiant, up to 25 people.

**Can we still get our licence at City Hall?**

Yes. As of November 20, 2026, the City still lists City Hall, first floor, for licence appointments. The licence is $180.

**Can we marry at home or in a park?**

Yes, with the owner's permission, a registered officiant and two witnesses.
