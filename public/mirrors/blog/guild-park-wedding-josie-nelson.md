Josie came up behind the bars in her gown, a step to his left, close enough to put a hand on the metal. Neither of them turned around. They found each other through the bars, and that was the first picture of the day.

Josie and Nelson married at [Guild Park & Gardens](/location-guide/guild-park/) on a Saturday at the end of August. I covered the day in photo and film. This is what a wedding there actually looks like from the inside.

## Guild Park & Gardens at a glance

**Location:** 201 Guildwood Parkway, Scarborough. The Guildwood neighbourhood, up on the Scarborough Bluffs in Toronto's east end.

**What it is:** A public park, free to walk into, open year round. Rosa and Spencer Clark ran the estate here as the Guild of All Arts from 1932 and spent decades rescuing stone from downtown buildings that were coming down.

**The ruins:** Over 70 architectural fragments from more than 60 Toronto buildings demolished after the Second World War. Columns, arches and carved facades standing in mown grass.

**The Greek Theatre:** Built in 1982 from eight marble Corinthian columns and the arches of the 1912 Bank of Toronto at King and Bay, torn down in 1966 for the Toronto-Dominion Centre.

**The venue inside the park:** The Guild Inn Estate, the restored 1914 Bickford House plus new wings, reopened in 2017. Seated ceremonies and receptions happen there, not on the public lawn.

**Getting there:** 25 to 35 minutes from downtown by car, free parking on site. The 116 Morningside bus runs along Guildwood Parkway, and Guildwood GO is about a 25 minute walk.

## Before the ceremony

Before the ceremony the garden split in two. Josie's bridesmaids in sage green held her bouquet up between them while she laughed at something off to the side. Out on the lawn, Nelson's groomsmen piled into him in a hug that turned into something closer to a tackle.

Then the garden went quiet for the two of them.

There is a wall of blush hydrangeas in the garden and we used all of it. Nelson tucked a loose strand of hair behind Josie's ear. They put their foreheads together and said nothing for a while.

![Josie and Nelson hand in hand in front of the carved caryatid balustrade at Guild Park, by AD Photography](../../assets/images/portfolio/josie-nelson-wedding/13.jpg)

A short walk away there is a stone balustrade with carved figures set into it, saved from a building downtown and now standing in grass. Josie's cascading orchid bouquet in front of old stone is the whole idea of this park in one frame.

Then the arch. Josie opened her veil wide underneath it and held it there, and the lace filled the whole opening.

![Josie holding her veil open under the stone arch at Guild Park & Gardens, by AD Photography](../../assets/images/portfolio/josie-nelson-wedding/16.jpg)

Her bridesmaids threw white rose petals at her after that, and she gathered the train in one hand and laughed her way through it.

## The ceremony

The ceremony ran under a white draped tent on the stone terrace at the Guild Inn Estate, a chandelier hanging in the middle of it, white and green florals at the posts.

![Josie escorted down the steps into the white draped ceremony tent by her parents, by AD Photography](../../assets/images/portfolio/josie-nelson-wedding/22.jpg)

Josie came down the steps between her parents, veil down, train following her. Nelson was already at the front. There is a frame of him waiting, taken between the shoulders of two seated guests, and he is not looking at anything except the top of those steps.

![Josie and Nelson at the front of the tent with her train fanned in a circle across the stone, by AD Photography](../../assets/images/portfolio/josie-nelson-wedding/26.jpg)

Then the part I keep. Josie read her vows through her veil, from a small booklet with the word Vows printed on the cover in gold. For a few minutes that veil covered both their faces at once, close enough that nobody else under the tent could hear a word of it. Nelson laughed in the middle of his own vows.

The ring went on and he kept hold of her hand after it was on. Then he lifted the veil.

![Nelson and Josie's first kiss under the ceremony tent as the officiant looks on, by AD Photography](../../assets/images/portfolio/josie-nelson-wedding/33.jpg)

They walked back up the aisle through bubbles, Josie lifting her train off the stone, and the tent stayed loud the whole way out.

## Portraits in the ruins

Once the ceremony is done the park belongs to you, and here that matters more than it does at most venues. No hotel corridor, no waiting on a lift. You step out of the tent and you are already in a garden full of rescued stone.

![Josie and Nelson framed inside the standing marble doorway at Guild Park with the lawn beyond, by AD Photography](../../assets/images/portfolio/josie-nelson-wedding/39.jpg)

A full marble doorway stands on its own in the grass. It used to be the way into a building downtown. They stood inside it and laughed at each other while the lawn ran away behind them.

Out on the open grass the sun sat low enough to draw an outline around both of them from behind. Josie changed into a second dress for the evening and we walked the allée, a long path with marble columns either side and flowerbeds running the length of it.

![Josie and Nelson walking the columned allée at Guild Park, Nelson waving, seen through foreground leaves, by AD Photography](../../assets/images/portfolio/josie-nelson-wedding/41.jpg)

## The tent, the toasts and the candles

![A dip and a kiss under the string lights in the reception tent at Guild Park, by AD Photography](../../assets/images/portfolio/josie-nelson-wedding/42.jpg)

The tent filled up and the string lights came on. Nelson pulled Josie into a dip underneath them, her bouquet hanging down green and long. At the head table they sat shoulder to shoulder, laughing at whatever the rest of the room was laughing at.

The speeches got them both. Nelson on the microphone, Josie beside him laughing over her own pages. Bottles and glasses met over the candles. Later, against the glow of the chandelier, the two of them turned into one dark shape.

Then the guests went outside and lit candles.

![Josie and Nelson kissing inside the candle tunnel as guests cheer outside the tent at night, by AD Photography](../../assets/images/portfolio/josie-nelson-wedding/50.jpg)

They made a tunnel along the path, two rows of raised flames in the dark. The couple walked into it and stopped halfway to kiss. That is the last frame in the gallery and it should be.

## If you are planning a wedding at Guild Park

Two things get mixed up here constantly.

**The park and the estate are separate bookings.** Guild Park & Gardens is City of Toronto parkland. The Guild Inn Estate is the event venue sitting inside it, and the estate is who you speak to about a seated ceremony and a dinner. Josie and Nelson's ceremony and reception were both at the estate.

**A ceremony in the public park comes with rules.** The City permits ceremonies in the park itself for up to 100 people, standing only. No chairs, no decorations, no confetti, no food and no drink. That covers a short outdoor ceremony and nothing more.

**Wedding photography in the park is its own booking.** The City lists it at $485.47 for two hours including tax, for up to 30 people, through Client Services Park Bookings at 416-396-7378. Engagement sessions and general photography need no permit, which is the part most couples do not expect.

**Timing.** In late summer, save the last hour of sun for the open lawn and the allée. Everything before it can live near the ruins and the hydrangeas, where the trees hold the light soft all afternoon.
