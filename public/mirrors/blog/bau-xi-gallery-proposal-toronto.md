C went down on one knee in the front room of Bau-Xi Gallery, between two tall arched windows. A put both hands on both cheeks and smiled the whole way through. There was nobody else in the room.

![C on one knee in the front room of Bau-Xi Gallery as A smiles with both hands on both cheeks between two tall arched windows, by AD Photography](../../assets/images/portfolio/c-a-bau-xi-gallery/01.jpg)

That was a Friday evening in early November, a little after half past six, at the Bau-Xi Gallery on Dundas Street West, right across the street from the Art Gallery of Ontario. Most people walk past it on the way to the AGO. It turned out to be one of the warmest, most private proposals I have photographed.

This post covers the gallery, the rooms I used, timing, and how to ask the gallery, with C and A's proposal as the example.

## Bau-Xi Gallery at a glance

**Location:** 340 Dundas Street West, directly across from the Art Gallery of Ontario.

**What it is:** A private gallery for contemporary painting, sculpture and photography. Bau-Xi opened in Vancouver in 1965. The Dundas gallery is now the downtown satellite to its bigger flagship on Dufferin Street.

**Setting:** White walls and track lights, honey maple floors in the main rooms, pale pine floors in the back, two tall arched windows, a carpeted stair to the upper rooms, and chrome and leather chairs. The art changes from show to show.

**Hours:** Tuesday to Saturday, 10 am to 5:30 pm. Closed Sunday, Monday and holidays.

**Events:** The gallery rents the Dundas space for small private functions, evenings until midnight. Contact the events manager at toronto@bau-xi.com or 416-977-0600.

**Getting there:** The 505 Dundas streetcar stops nearby. St Patrick station is a short walk east.

## Why a gallery works for a proposal

**It is warm.** C and A's proposal was in November. In a park that means grey light, wind and a coat over the outfit. In here A wore a simple black slip dress the whole hour.

**It is quiet.** With the rooms empty there is no crowd to hide from and nobody walking through the frame.

**The walls do the work.** Every room has big colour on white walls. You do not need flowers, signs or a setup. The paintings are the setup.

![C and A holding hands in front of a large blue and peach abstract painting, A looking back over a shoulder, by AD Photography](../../assets/images/portfolio/c-a-bau-xi-gallery/03.jpg)

## The best rooms after the yes

### 1. The front room with the arched windows

This is where the proposal happened. The two arched windows give the room a centre, so the person kneeling and the person standing both sit in a clean, simple frame. Stay here a few minutes after the yes.

### 2. The big abstract wall

Right next to the windows hangs a huge blue and peach abstract. Two people holding hands in front of it, seen from behind, makes a quiet frame that looks like it belongs in the show.

![C and A hand in hand, seen from behind, in front of a bright yellow and black abstract canvas at Bau-Xi Gallery, by AD Photography](../../assets/images/portfolio/c-a-bau-xi-gallery/04.jpg)

### 3. The stairs

There is a carpeted stair to the upper rooms. At the top, a tall painting of green and gold leaves fills the wall, and it frames a kiss from the side.

![A kiss at the top of the gallery stairs beside a tall painting of green and gold leaves, by AD Photography](../../assets/images/portfolio/c-a-bau-xi-gallery/05.jpg)

### 4. The back room with the pine floor

This room has a pale pine floor and a lot of open space. It is the room for movement: a slow dance, a twirl, a dip. A few of these frames are soft with motion on purpose, and one is in black and white.

![C dipping A into a kiss on the pine floor of Bau-Xi Gallery beneath a dark painting of white flowers, by AD Photography](../../assets/images/portfolio/c-a-bau-xi-gallery/11.jpg)

### 5. The ring against the art

Look for a piece with a plain, dark background. A had a green stone ring, and their hands went up in front of a picture of yellow flowers in a dark vase so the ring stood out.

![C and A holding hands in front of an artwork of pale yellow flowers in a dark vase, the green ring in focus, by AD Photography](../../assets/images/portfolio/c-a-bau-xi-gallery/15.jpg)

### 6. The main room, sitting down

We finished in the biggest room. Two chrome chairs in the middle of the floor and a glass each. Then the carpeted step at the edge of the floor, with the arched windows behind them.

![A laughing with chin on hand, a glass of bubbly in the other hand, as C leans in from the next chair at Bau-Xi Gallery, by AD Photography](../../assets/images/portfolio/c-a-bau-xi-gallery/18.jpg)

## How the proposal worked

**Pick the exact spot.** For C and A it was the front room, between the arched windows. Not "somewhere in the gallery".

**The gallery was empty.** We started a little after 6:30 pm on a Friday, after the posted closing time, so there were no other visitors.

**I stayed out of the way.** I photographed the knee and the first reaction from behind C, then came in close after the yes.

**Then about twenty five minutes of portraits.** Room by room: the abstracts, the stairs, the back room, the ring, the chairs. A small gallery is enough for a full set because every wall looks different.

![A laughing over C's shoulder and holding up the ringed hand toward the camera, by AD Photography](../../assets/images/portfolio/c-a-bau-xi-gallery/29.jpg)

The full set is in the [C and A gallery](/portfolio/c-a-bau-xi-gallery/).

## Asking the gallery

**This is private property.** A gallery is not a City park, so there is no City permit. The only yes you need, other than the one you are asking for, is the gallery's.

**Talk to the events manager.** Bau-Xi lists the Dundas space for small private functions, with evening rentals until midnight. Email toronto@bau-xi.com or call 416-977-0600, say it is a proposal, and ask what they allow and what it costs.

**Be clear about the size.** Two people and one photographer. No lights on stands, no setup on the walls, nothing that touches the art.

**Respect the work.** Do not lean on or touch the paintings, and keep drinks well away from them.

## When to go

**After closing** is the best time, if the gallery agrees. Empty rooms make the whole hour feel like yours.

**Late fall and winter** are when a gallery makes the most sense. When the parks are bare and cold, the colour is all inside.

**Check the current show.** The art on the walls changes. Look at what is hanging the week of your date so you know what colours you are working with.

## Who a gallery proposal is right for

**You both love art.** Or you met at a show, or you always end up in the AGO. Proposing across the street feels like it means something.

**You want private.** No crowds, no strangers in the background, no hiding behind a tree.

**Your date is in the cold months.** If you are planning for November to March, this is a much kinder plan than a windy waterfront.
