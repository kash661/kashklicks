## Fall engagement photos in Toronto at a glance

**Best weeks:** Mid to late October in a typical year. High Park usually peaks in the last two weeks of October, right after Thanksgiving.

**Where:** High Park for colour, RC Harris for stone and lake, and the waterfront at Biidaasige Park for the skyline after dark.

**Best light:** The sun sits lower in fall, so the light stays softer for more of the day. A grey day gives you soft, even light from start to finish.

**Sunset:** About 6:35 PM in mid October and about 6:10 PM at the end of the month. Clocks go back on Sunday, November 1, 2026, and after that sunset is just after 5 PM.

**Permits:** None for a small session at RC Harris. In City parks like High Park, a couple with one photographer is usually everyday park use, and my [Toronto photo permits table](/toronto-photo-permits/) lists each one.

**What to wear:** Soft neutrals or one deep colour. Let the leaves do the bright part.

**Cost:** Sessions start at $351.25. A day with more than one location needs a bigger package, from $551.25.

## Why fall is worth planning around

The colour does not wait for you. At High Park the peak usually fills the last two weeks of October, and then the leaves come down.

The sun sits lower, so the light is softer for more of the day, and you are not waiting for one golden hour at the end. It is cool enough for layers. And it gets dark early enough that a night skyline chapter fits into the same day. Harnoor and Harsh's last frame was at 8:22 PM.

The trade off is real. The window is short, and fall weather in Toronto is a coin toss. If that worries you, read my [rainy day engagement photos guide](/blog/rainy-day-engagement-photos-toronto/) before you pick a date.

![Harsh kneels to kiss Harnoor's hand at Biidaasige Park, the CN Tower lit across the water, by AD Photography](../../assets/images/portfolio/harnoor-harsh/42.jpg)

## When to book: the fall colour clock

Couples ask me when the leaves change in Toronto. It moves every year, but this is how I plan it.

**Late September to early October.** Mostly green, with first colour in early places like the ivy at U of T. Sunset is a little later than in mid October.

**Mid October.** Maples going red and orange, plenty of green still around. This is where Harnoor and Harsh's October 11 session sat. Sunset is about 6:35 PM.

**The last two weeks of October.** The usual High Park peak, right after Thanksgiving. High Park tends to turn a little later than the Don Valley. Sunset moves toward 6:10 PM by the end of the month.

**Early November.** Whatever colour is left, and a short day. Clocks go back on Sunday, November 1, 2026, and after that sunset is just after 5 PM.

My advice is to pick a date in your window and hold a backup date next to it. If heavy rain or a storm is coming, we move.

## Permits and parking

**RC Harris.** No permit is needed for a small couple session on the public exterior grounds. The interiors and fenced zones are off limits. Street parking nearby is free until 4 PM, which suits a morning start.

**High Park and Biidaasige Park.** Both are City of Toronto parks. A couple walking a park with one photographer and a handheld camera is treated as everyday park use at most City parks. A permit comes in for formal or wedding photography, or for equipment, staging or a big group. My [Toronto photo permits table](/toronto-photo-permits/) marks High Park as "sometimes" for that reason, so I check your plan against it before the day. If you do need one, my [park permit booking guide](/blog/toronto-park-photo-permit-how-to-book/) walks you through the call. At High Park there is street parking around the edge and a few small paid lots inside.
