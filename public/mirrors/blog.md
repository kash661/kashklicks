## About the Journal

AD Photography is a solo, Toronto-based wedding and pre-wedding photography and videography studio. The journal collects the practical things couples ask before and during their shoots: where to shoot in the GTA, what venues feel like to photograph at, what to wear, how to plan timelines, and what to expect on the day. Written by Akash, who photographs every session.
