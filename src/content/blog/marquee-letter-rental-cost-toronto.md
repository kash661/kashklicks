## What most GTA rental companies charge

Most companies price marquee letters one piece at a time, and $89 for a 4 ft letter or number is the price you see again and again. Published prices sit between about $79 and $100.

Common words like LOVE also come as a set, which can cost a little less. Four letters at $89 would be $356, and published LOVE sets start at $300. OH BABY sets list at $475 to $550, and two numbers at $175 to $190. Every one of those prices is before delivery.

## Delivery is the price most companies leave out

Look at almost any marquee letter listing in the GTA and you will find the same phrase: $89 plus delivery. The delivery price is usually missing because it is quoted by address. It depends on how far your venue is from the company's warehouse, and on how easy the venue is to get into. Stairs, no freight elevator or nowhere close to park can all add to it.

A few companies do publish their delivery prices, and they show how fast the number grows. These are examples, not averages.

- A rental company in Durham that drops off at the curb, with no setup, charges $70 to Ajax or Whitby, $100 to Scarborough or Markham, and $130 to Toronto.
- A decor company in Hamilton charges $275 for delivery, setup and pickup within 60 km of its warehouse, plus $1.50 per km past about 70 km.
- At one Toronto company, orders under $200 are pickup only. It charges $100 if a pickup fails and moves to the next day, and adds a fee for stairs or a venue with no freight elevator.
- Late pickup fees, where published, run from $75 after 1 am at one company to $150 after 11 pm at another.

Picking the letters up yourself skips the delivery fee, but not the effort. One company holds a deposit of $100 to $150 per letter when you collect. You also need a vehicle with room for letters that stand 4 ft tall, and time for two round trips.

That is not a knock on those companies. Delivery really does change with the address. It just means the $89 on a listing is not the whole bill unless you collect the letters yourself.

## What changes the price of marquee letters

Beyond delivery and late pickups, these also move a marquee letter quote across the GTA.

**How many pieces.** Most companies price per letter, so every piece adds to the bill. A set can come in a little under the per letter rate.

**Colour.** One Toronto company lists coloured or black 4 ft letters at $135 to $160 each, against about $89 for a standard letter.

**Size.** 4 ft is the standard. The companies we found with 5 ft letters quote them on request and publish no price.

**Order size.** At one Toronto company, orders under $200 are pickup only. Another only delivers free once an order passes $1,000.

With us, three things change the price: an extra number, a pickup after 11 pm, and delivery outside the free zone.

## Questions to ask before you book any marquee letter rental

Whoever you rent from, these questions turn a listing price into a real total.

- Is delivery included, and does it include setup, or is it a drop at the curb?
- What time do you pick up, and what does a late pickup cost?
- Is there a fee for stairs or a venue with no freight elevator?
- Are extension cords included, and how close does a wall outlet need to be?
- Can the letters go outside?
- If I collect them myself, how much is the deposit, and when do I get it back?
- What is the full total for my address and my date?
