## Eight wedding marquee letter ideas using LOVE

Here is each spot the way I see it from behind the camera, with the details that decide whether it works.

### 1. Behind the sweetheart or head table

Speeches and dinner keep you at this table for a long stretch of the night, and a photographer's camera keeps coming back to it. With LOVE behind you, nearly every photo of the two of you at the table carries the word.

A few details make it work. Leave a walkway between the table and the letters, so people can reach their seats without brushing past the bulbs. Keep tall florals and centrepieces low, or move them to the ends, so they do not cover the middle of the word. And if the table sits on a raised stage, tell us when you book, because letters on the floor below a stage lose most of their height behind it.

### 2. At the edge of the dance floor

Put LOVE at the edge of the dance floor, never on it. When the room goes dark for the first dance, the bulbs become part of the light, and the wide shots of that dance show the word glowing behind you.

Keep the letters clear of the DJ's speakers and cables. And decide one thing in advance: coloured DJ lighting washes over everything near it, so the letters can photograph pink or blue instead of warm white. If you want LOVE to stay warm, ask your DJ to keep the colour off that side of the room.

### 3. A photo corner guests will actually use

A photo corner only works if people use it. Give it open floor in front, enough for a group of friends and for your photographer to step back. Give it some soft light on faces, because the bulbs light the word, not your guests. And put it away from the exits and the bar line, so nobody queues through the photos. Close to a wall outlet is best, so the cords stay short and out of the way.

### 4. At the entrance or cocktail hour

LOVE at the entrance tells guests they are in the right room before anyone says hello. The honest catch is light. The glow looks weak in daylight and beside big windows, and it grows stronger as the evening goes. In summer, Toronto stays bright well past cocktail hour, so a spot by the windows will not show the letters at their best. An indoor foyer or an inside wall beats a sunny patio, and our letters are made for indoor use anyway.

The letters also stay in one spot for the night, so choose the entrance only if you are happy to leave LOVE there after dinner.

### 5. Behind the cake table

The cake cutting is one of the moments guests reach for their phones. Put the letters behind the cake table, not beside it, so the cake and LOVE share one frame. If the cake is tall, set it slightly off centre so it does not hide the middle of the word. Then cut from the side of the table rather than from behind it, so the two of you are not standing in front of the letters.

### 6. Framing an indoor ceremony

For a ceremony in a banquet hall, a hotel ballroom or a restaurant's private room, LOVE can frame the vows. Set it to one side of where you will stand, not directly behind you. At 4 ft, the letters sit lower than a standing couple and officiant, so directly behind the three of you they end up partly hidden in most ceremony photos. Off to one side, the word shows in the wide shots and in the walk back down the aisle.

If the same room turns into the reception, pick a spot that works for both.

### 7. The nights around the wedding

A wedding can stretch across several nights: an engagement party, a bridal shower, a mehndi or sangeet, a rehearsal dinner, or a party held weeks after a [City Hall ceremony](/blog/toronto-city-hall-wedding-photographer-guide/). ## What size marquee letters work for a wedding

Big light up letters for rent usually come in 3, 4 or 5 ft. Most GTA rental companies list 4 ft as their standard size, and every letter and number we rent stands 4 ft tall. That is roughly chest height on most adults, which is why the letters read well behind a table and why anyone standing directly in front of the word hides part of it.

Before your floor plan is final, plan for these:

**Width.** Send us a photo of the spot and we will confirm LOVE fits before you book.

**Floor.** The letters need flat, level floor. Stage edges, steps and slopes are not a spot for them unless we have confirmed it with you.

**Room in front.** Leave open floor in front of the letters so guests can gather and your photographer can step back far enough to fit the whole word.

**One spot for the night.** Plan on the letters staying where we set them. If you want them moved during the event, ask us before you book.

**Your venue.** Tell your venue coordinator the letters are coming. Supplier access times and decor rules differ from one venue to the next, whether it is a banquet hall in Vaughan or a hotel ballroom downtown. And tell us about stairs, elevators or a long walk from the loading door, so nothing surprises anyone on the night.

## How to photograph marquee letters at night

The short version from behind the camera:

- **Dim the room.** The glow is strongest once the house lights come down. In a bright room the bulbs barely register.
- **Stand beside the word, not in front of it.** At 4 ft, anyone standing in front hides a letter or two. Stand to one side, a step or two forward rather than pressed against the bulbs.
- **Keep the whole word in frame.** Crop off the E and your photo says LOV.
- **Expose for faces, not bulbs.** Ask your photographer to set the exposure for your faces and let the bulbs glow. On a phone, tap a face, then slide the exposure down a touch so the bulbs keep their shape.
- **Skip the phone flash.** It flattens the glow.
- **Stripes mean flicker.** If faint stripes run across a photo, a light in the room is flickering faster than your eye can see. Take a few more frames. A photographer can usually clear it by slowing the shutter to 1/60 of a second.
