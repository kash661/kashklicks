There is a window of about thirty minutes after sunrise when Lake Ontario does something we can't replicate any other time of day. The light comes in low across the water, the lake usually holds a touch of mist, and the whole shoreline turns warm gold. We've shot a lot of pre-wedding and engagement sessions at this hour, and the truth is the location matters more here than at any other time of day. You need a spot that faces the right direction, that we can actually get to before the sun comes up, and that gives us something to do with our hands while we wait for the light.

Here are our five favourite sunrise photo shoot locations along the Lake Ontario shoreline, ordered from east to west.

## 1. Scarborough Bluffs

**Vibe:** Cinematic, dramatic, like shooting on a cliffside set you didn't have to build

The Bluffs are the most theatrical sunrise location in the GTA. We almost always shoot from Bluffer's Park at lake level rather than from the clifftop parks. From the beach side you get the sun coming up over the water on your left, and the white clay cliffs rising behind you catch the first warm light a few minutes after the sun clears the horizon. That tiny gap between sun-on-water and sun-on-cliff is the magic window.

**Best time to shoot:** Arrive 25 minutes before sunrise to claim the spit of beach at the east end of Bluffer's Park. The cliffs go from blue to peach to fully lit in about fifteen minutes.

**Parking:** Bluffer's Park has a large parking lot at the bottom of Brimley Road. It's paid in season but at sunrise you'll have most of it to yourself.

**Pro tip:** Bring footwear you can get wet. The best frames are right at the waterline, and the sand near the cliffs is soft and damp. We always pack a microfibre towel for the couple's shoes.

We've put together a fuller breakdown of the Bluffs on our [Scarborough Bluffs location guide](/location-guide/scarborough-bluffs/), including notes on the clifftop spots and what each one offers.

## 2. The Beaches and Leuty Lifeguard Station

**Vibe:** Quiet east-end Toronto, boardwalk, red-roofed lifeguard station that everyone recognizes

The Beaches boardwalk runs along the south edge of Toronto from Woodbine all the way past Kew Gardens, and at sunrise the whole stretch is yours. We always anchor our shoots around the Leuty Lifeguard Station, the white-and-red wooden building near the Maclean Avenue access point. It is the most photographed landmark in this neighbourhood for good reason. Position the couple on the boardwalk with the lifeguard station to one side and the lake glowing to the south-east, and you have an image that reads as Toronto without needing a caption.

**Best time to shoot:** First twenty minutes after sunrise. The boardwalk fills with runners and dogs by 7am on weekends, so we are usually wrapping by then.

**Parking:** Free street parking on Lake Shore Boulevard East and along the side streets south of Queen East before 8am. Lots fill quickly after.

**Pro tip:** The stretch around Balmy Beach, east of the lifeguard station, catches the sunrise more directly than the west end near Woodbine. Walk east if you want the sun fully in the frame.

## 3. Humber Bay Park East

**Vibe:** Iconic Toronto skyline, mirror-still pond, postcard sunrise

This is the spot we go to when a couple says they want "that Toronto sunrise photo." Humber Bay Park East is a small peninsula on the west side of the city, and at the east edge of the park there is a small pond and shoreline that frames the entire downtown skyline across the water. At sunrise the sun comes up behind and to the right of the CN Tower, and within a few minutes the west faces of the glass towers light up and reflect into the pond if the water is calm.

The [Humber Bay Arch Bridge](/location-guide/humber-bay-arch-bridge/) is a short walk away if you want a second backdrop in the same session.

**Best time to shoot:** Five to fifteen minutes after sunrise, when the skyline glass starts to glow but the sky still holds blue. The pond reflection holds best before any wind picks up.

**Parking:** Free lot off Marine Parade Drive. Walk five minutes east along the path to the pond.

**Pro tip:** Bring a wide lens. The skyline is far enough away that a 24mm gives you the whole frame including the pond reflection. A 35mm crops out the corners.

## 4. Adamson Estate

**Vibe:** Heritage mansion, lakefront lawn, Mississauga hush

![A couple on the lakefront lawn at Adamson Estate looking out over Lake Ontario beneath a soft dawn sky, by AD Photography](../../assets/images/portfolio/ayushi-parth-adamson/06.jpg)

[Adamson Estate](/location-guide/adamson-estate/) is one of our quietest discoveries. The grounds run right to the edge of Lake Ontario in Mississauga's Lakeview neighbourhood, and the historic mansion sits set back behind a long lawn that opens straight onto the water. At sunrise the lawn fills with sidelight coming in from the east, and the mansion's east-facing wing warms up a few minutes later. We usually shoot the lake side first and the architecture second.

**Best time to shoot:** From first light through about thirty minutes after sunrise. The lawn glows, then the mansion glows, then the light flattens out.

**Parking:** Free lot on the west side of the property off Lakeshore Road East.

**Pro tip:** The dock and shoreline path on the lake side of the property is the most underused spot. We always end the session there with a wider environmental frame of the couple, the lake, and the soft pink sky.

## 5. Paletta Lakefront Park

**Vibe:** Stone heritage mansion, a creek that meets the lake, English-garden feeling

The drive to Burlington is worth it. Paletta Lakefront Park is a fourteen-acre heritage park at 4250 Lakeshore Road, sitting at the mouth of Shoreacres Creek where the creek opens into Lake Ontario. A stone mansion overlooks both. We shoot the couple on the mansion's east lawn at first light, then move down the path to the creek mouth, then finish on the lakefront. Three different backdrops in one park, all within a five-minute walk, all catching sunrise from a different angle.

**Best time to shoot:** First light through forty minutes after sunrise. The mansion needs the sun a little higher to fully light its east face, so we usually do the lake first and the building second.

**Parking:** Free public lot at the entrance. Verify current park hours with the City of Burlington before planning a pre-dawn arrival; posted hours start at 8am.

**Pro tip:** The little stone bridge over the creek inside the park is one of our favourite intimate frames. It is tucked enough that you forget you are inside a busy public park.
